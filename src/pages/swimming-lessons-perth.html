How our lessons work

What your child gets in a lesson

Four kids per class, at most

A 30-minute class isn't long, and four is the most one instructor can properly coach. We've been running since 2019 with more than 1,200 swimmers, and have never had a learn-to-swim class with more than four kids in it. Why four.

Correct technique from the first lesson

We start with body position, head position and how your child engages with the water. Teaching it right the first time is far easier than fixing bad habits later. Most kids who start with us at 4 or 5 are swimming 1–2km with efficient technique by 8 or 9.

Instructors who swim

Every instructor is a current or former squad swimmer, many from our own squads. The program was designed by Head Coach JJ Williams with Dr. Nigel Williams, a child and adolescent behaviour specialist, and runs on process, not pressure. Meet the team.

No level system

Kids don't get stuck waiting to pass one skill. Your child stays with one instructor who teaches similar-ability kids, builds on what they do well and helps with what they find hard. How that works.
